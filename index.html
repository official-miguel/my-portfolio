About me

Software that's running in real schools.

I've been building software since 2022, starting with things people around me needed. The biggest is Bidii, a school management system with M-Pesa fee payments, report sheets, timetables and an offline mobile app. Schools in Kenya now use it to run their day.

Through Trillionaire Designs I also build apps for clients, including activation-code platforms, bookkeeping tools for small shops and business websites. I handle all of it myself: design, database, payments, deployment and monitoring.

If you need a developer who can turn an idea into something your customers rely on, I'm available for full-time roles and freelance projects, remote or in Nairobi.
